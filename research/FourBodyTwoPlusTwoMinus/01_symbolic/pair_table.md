# Pair analysis (2+/2- with q1=q2=+1, q3=q4=-1, m=c=1)

| pair (i,j) | q_i q_j | Coulomb | Weber correction sign | ρ_ij = q_i q_j /(μ c²), μ=1/2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (1,2) | 1 | repulsive (+1/r) | negative (lowers H; weakens repulsion) | +2 |
| (1,3) | -1 | attractive (-1/r) | positive (raises H; weakens attraction) | -2 |
| (1,4) | -1 | attractive (-1/r) | positive (raises H; weakens attraction) | -2 |
| (2,3) | -1 | attractive (-1/r) | positive (raises H; weakens attraction) | -2 |
| (2,4) | -1 | attractive (-1/r) | positive (raises H; weakens attraction) | -2 |
| (3,4) | 1 | repulsive (+1/r) | negative (lowers H; weakens repulsion) | +2 |

With μ_ij = m_i m_j/(m_i+m_j) = 1/2 and c=1: ρ_ij = 2·q_i q_j ∈ {+2,-2}.
Like-charge pairs (1,2),(3,4) have ρ = +2; the four unlike-sign pairs have ρ = -2.
